Privacy Policy

DIETARY MANAGEMENT (the "App") / Last updated: September 19, 2026

The App does not collect any personal information. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect or transmit any information about you. The developer cannot access or obtain your data.

The App contains no analytics tools, no advertising networks, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) of any kind.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Everything you enter in the App — meal records, body weight, nutrient goals, information about family members you add, and foods and recipes you register — is stored only on your device. None of it is sent to any server.

This data leaves your device only if you have enabled Apple's backup features (such as iCloud Backup), in which case it may be included in those backups and handled according to Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting the App from your device deletes the data stored in it. The developer retains no copy.

3. Health-Related Information

The meal records and body weight the App handles are stored only on your device, as described above. The App does not integrate with Apple Health (HealthKit) and neither reads nor writes health data belonging to other apps.

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

All purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. Payment details such as your name, address, and credit card information are handled by Apple and are never provided to the developer.

The App determines whether a subscription is active on your device using Apple's StoreKit framework. No personally identifying information is used in this process.

5. Notifications

All notifications shown by the App are generated on your device. No communication with any external service takes place for notifications.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties

Because no information is collected, there is no information to disclose to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children. As stated above, the App collects no information from any user, regardless of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

If a future feature changes how information is handled, an updated version of this policy will be published on this page before the release that contains that feature.
