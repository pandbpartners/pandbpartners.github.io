Privacy Policy

Inscribit (the “App”) / Last updated: October 7, 2026

The App does not collect your personal information. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect or send any information about you. The developer never sees or obtains your data.

The App contains no analytics tools, no advertising networks, and no other third-party software (SDKs).

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Everything you enter in the App, including items, records, memos, and photos, is stored on your device.

If you use iCloud on your device, this data is also stored in your own private iCloud storage so that it stays in sync across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Only you can access this data; the developer cannot see or obtain it. It is handled under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

When you delete a record or an item in the App, it is deleted from all of your devices and from iCloud. Deleting the App removes the data from that device. Data stored in iCloud can be deleted from your iCloud storage settings on your device.

3. Photos and the Camera

When you add a photo from your library, the App receives only the photos you choose. It does not access the rest of your photo library.

The camera is used only when you take a photo or scan a document in the App.

Photos you add from your library are saved as they are, including any information they contain, such as the location where they were taken. This information stays within the App and your own iCloud storage and is never sent anywhere else.

4. App Lock

If you turn on the App Lock, Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode is checked by iOS. The App only learns whether you unlocked it; it never receives your face or fingerprint data or your passcode.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

All purchases in the App are made through Apple’s App Store. Your payment information, such as your name, address, and credit card details, is handled by Apple and is not provided to the developer.

The App checks whether your subscription is active on your device using Apple’s StoreKit. No personally identifiable information is used for this.

6. When You Contact Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. The email the App prepares for you includes the versions of the App and of iOS, which you can see and remove before sending. We use this information only to reply to you.

7. Sharing with Third Parties

Since the App does not collect your information, there is nothing to share with third parties.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children. As described above, the App does not collect information from any user, regardless of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the way the App handles information changes as features are added, we will publish an updated policy on this page before releasing the version that includes those features.

10. Contact

For questions, bug reports, or requests about the App, or inquiries about this policy, please contact us at:
